# Scouts South Africa — Brand Guidelines

Reference: [SCOUTS SA Marketing Toolkit](https://scoutwiki.scouts.org.za/wiki/SCOUTS_SA_Marketing_Toolkit)

## Logo Usage

### Badge Logo (Fleur-de-lis)

- The badge logo is available to registered, affiliated Scout Groups.
- Request via [email protected] — registration and affiliation must be confirmed before distribution.

### Corporate Brand (Purple & White with Rope)

- Restricted to **National and Regional teams** for official communication, warrants, awards, and merchandise.
- Exception: signage and teardrop banners.
- Scout Groups needing corporate brand materials must get **prior email approval** from [email protected].

### Trademark

> The World Scout Emblem (fleur-de-lis) and SCOUTS South Africa brand are registered trademarks owned by World Scouting. Materials may only be used in line with brand directives — for promotion of Scouting and not for any commercial gain.

### Rules

- Use the unified brand consistently across Groups, Districts, Regions, and events.
- Do **not** alter, amend, or recreate any logo without approval.
- Do **not** use any brand asset for commercial purposes.
- Maintain brand integrity — apply due diligence for correct visual representation.

## Background Textures

Available hessian/canvas textures for promotional materials:

- Cream (low-res and high-res)
- Light brown
- Dark brown
- Purple hessian
- Hessian with brown border

## Brand Tone

The brand conveys the **outdoor, adventurous, and fun nature of Scouting** (developed with agency HAVAS). Photography and imagery should reflect this.
